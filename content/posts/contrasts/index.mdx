Began more experimenting with how the Minolta handles shadows and lighting. I enjoyed setting some of these scenes intentionally. I discovered there's an 'ah-a' sensation you feel when you set the scene and line it up in the viewfinder just right. I do claim the latte art as my own (took me 9 months to get some consistency). I almost tossed the shed photo but something about it still intrigues me. It's got an aging nature, and the light leak gives a ghostly ephemeral feel as the subject is being erased.
