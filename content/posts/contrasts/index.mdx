Began more experimenting with how this camera handles shadows and lighting. I'm finding I enjoy setting some of these scenes intentionally. There's a visceral instinct you can feel when something lines up in the viewfinder 'just right'. I do claim the latte art as my own (took me 9 months to get consistency). I almost tossed the shed photo but something about it sticks with me. It's already got an aging posture, but the light leak adds a ghostly ephemeral feel as it erases the subject. I did replace the foam seals so hopefully that helps the light leak for future shoots.
